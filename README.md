# Absolute Septic: concept site by LuminArch

A pitch demo for Absolute Septic, LLC (Clayton, Smithfield and Garner, NC). **Not the official site.** Noindexed, blocked in robots.txt, and labeled with a concept banner on every page.

Absolute Septic's own site, absolute-septic.com, returns a 404 on every page as of 8 October 2026 (it was archived live as late as 7 June 2026). Content and photos here come from the archived copy, the state certification list, BBB, HomeAdvisor and Google.

- Live preview: https://daveo820.github.io/absolute-septic-demo/
- Pages: home, services, reviews, about, get a quote, 404
- Stack: static HTML, CSS and one small JS file, generated by `build.py` (run `python3 build.py`).
- Facts and sources: `FACTS_LOG.md`. Design: `DESIGN_DNA.md`. Audit and competitors: `market-research.md`.
- Screenshots: `screenshots/index-desktop-fold.png` and `screenshots/index-mobile-fold.png`.

Before launch: Cody confirms hours, phone numbers and the NCS number, the placeholders are filled, the form gets connected, and the concept banner and noindex come off.

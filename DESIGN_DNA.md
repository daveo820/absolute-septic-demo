# Design DNA: Absolute Septic concept

- **Archetype:** edge bleed split. A black slab hero with condensed caps on the left and Absolute Septic's own job photo running to the right edge of the screen, full height, with the green pipe dropping down the seam between them. Not used in any earlier LuminArch demo.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (800 to 900, all caps) for display, the way lettering reads on a truck. Work Sans for body at 18px. Neither was used before.
- **Color story (from the logo, the truck and their job photos):** tank `#0e100d` (logo black), slab `#1a1d18`, logo `#2cbf10` (logo green, buttons with black text), hose `#276b17` (green text on light), clay `#a8471f` (Johnston County red clay from the drain field photos), sand `#f3efe6` (page), gravel `#e1dbcf` (panels), ink `#1d201b`, plus a yellow placeholder tag.
- **Signature element:** the pipe. A thick hose green run with a rounded elbow. It drops down the hero seam, hangs off the inner page heads, turns into the service "line" (a vertical pipe with joints for each service), marks every kicker as a small elbow, and ends at the footer "outlet".
- **Motion personality:** flow. Pipes draw themselves in (stroke offset) once on view; blocks fade and rise 22px on a fast ease out. Off under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Grid breaks:** home, the mulching photo overlaps the clay panel by 40px with a hard black shadow; about, the truck photo hangs 140px out of the black head.
- **Footer:** "the outlet". The pipe ends over CALL THE TRUCK. in giant caps, the number in logo green, and six facts in a ruled grid.
- **Distinct from earlier demos:** black and green utility register vs Carroll's light navy, Wolf Den's dark red magazine and Triple Crown's navy and gold; hard 2px borders and offset shadows vs soft cards; condensed truck caps vs serif or wide grotesk display.
